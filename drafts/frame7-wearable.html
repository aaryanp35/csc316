Thrown out, still wearable

Draft design for the next milestone. Each icon shows wear for its grade (stains, holes, rips, frayed hems). Icons will drop into the bin on scroll; hovering a condition band highlights its garments and shows the count. Source: Norway post-consumer garment data (Zenodo 20440761), "textile waste, not for reuse" stream, condition graded 1–5.
